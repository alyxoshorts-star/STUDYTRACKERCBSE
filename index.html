🔬 Science: Electricity Mind Map
  • Ohm's Law: V = I R
  • Resistivity (ρ): R = ρ L / A (Depends on temperature & material)
  • Series Combination: R_eq = R₁ + R₂ + R₃
  • Parallel Combination: 1/R_eq = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂
  • Joule's Heating: H = I² R t
📐 Mathematics: Real Numbers & Algebra
  • Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic: Composite = Product of Primes
  • HCF × LCM: a × b = HCF(a,b) × LCM(a,b)
  • Quadratic Formula: x = [-b ± √(b² - 4ac)] / 2a
  • Discriminant (D): D > 0 (2 distinct real roots), D = 0 (equal roots)
